# Turtle Strategy Brief for Life Coach

## What Will Is Doing

Will sells weekly $10 OTM call options against long-dated LEAPS positions (240-300 DTE, 85-90 delta) on SPY. Each Monday, he collects premium (~$200/week per contract) while the LEAPS provides directional protection. The goal is to:

1. **Generate monthly income** - Premium collected ($800/month) minus LEAPS decay ($240/month) = $560/month withdrawable per contract
2. **Capital appreciation** - LEAPS value grows with market over time
3. **Scale to 10 contracts** - Building to $5,600/month passive income ($67k annually)

The strategy works because institutions desperately need volatility protection and Will has the patience/flexibility they lack. He's selling insurance to giants who must buy it regardless of price.

## Capital Requirements

- **Per contract needs:** $24,000 total ($12k LEAPS, $10k rolling reserve, $2k buffer)
- **Current goal:** Scale from 1-2 contracts → 10 contracts ($240k deployment)
- **2x capital rule:** Never deploy more than 50% in LEAPS, always maintain reserves for rolling

## His Historical Pattern (The Problem)

**Will's documented failures all stem from emotional decision-making during market stress:**

1. **April 2025:** LEAPS delta collapsed to 0.60-0.70, didn't roll, lost money in recovering market
2. **Google disaster:** Panic-sold LEAPS during drawdown, missed recovery from $120 → $220 (left $48k on table)
3. **Summer 2024:** Got distracted by iron condors and other "sophisticated" strategies while profitable turtle account suffered
4. **2025 generally:** Went to cash during corrections exactly when elevated premiums offered maximum profit

**The pattern:** Strategy works → gets boring → seeks excitement → deviates → loses money → returns to turtle → repeat

## The System He Must Follow

### VIX-Based Decision Framework

**VIX is the primary indicator** (not price, not gut feeling):

| VIX Level | Action | Strike Selection |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| <20 | Normal ops | $10 OTM |
| 20-30 rising | Elevated stress | $2-7 OTM, roll if delta <0.72 |
| 30-50 rising | Crisis - sell ITM | ATM to 5 ITM for protection |
| >30 declining 10+ points | **STOP SELLING** | Go naked - recovery mode |
| <30 declining | Resume gradually | $15 OTM → $10 OTM as normalizes |

### The Critical Rules

1. **Every Monday: Check VIX, execute the prescribed action** - No skipping, no "market timing"
2. **Delta <0.72 = Roll down immediately** (if cash available) - Restore hedge, costs $2-3k
3. **VIX declining from peak = STOP selling** - Recovery rallies will hurt broken-delta positions
4. **Never go to cash during drawdowns** - LEAPS + time = recovery (Google lesson)
5. **$10 OTM is the standard** - Systematic, not discretionary
6. **240-300 DTE LEAPS** - Time is the safety net that lets you survive any crash

## The Psychological Traps (Coach Must Watch For)

### 1. Strategy Drift
**Symptoms:** Talking about new strategies, iron condors, bull spreads, "just exploring"
**Reality:** Boredom seeking entertainment disguised as optimization
**Coach response:** "Will this beat weekly calls on LEAPS? No? Then it's a distraction."

### 2. Panic Exit During Corrections
**Symptoms:** "Market looks terrible, maybe I should go to cash," "VIX at 35, this feels dangerous"
**Reality:** Institutional panic = maximum profit opportunity. VIX >30 means $400-600/week premiums
**Coach response:** "Remember Google. Going to cash cost you $48k. What's different this time?"

### 3. Optimization Theater
**Symptoms:** "Should I sell $8 OTM or $12 OTM?" "Maybe ATM is better this week"
**Reality:** Discretionary decisions override systematic edge
**Coach response:** "The playbook says $10 OTM. Are you following the system or your gut?"

### 4. Rolling Avoidance
**Symptoms:** Delta drops to 0.68, "maybe it'll recover," delays rolling
**Reality:** Broken hedge means losing money on UP moves (documented April 2025 mistake)
**Coach response:** "Delta <0.72 means roll. You have reserves. Why are you hesitating?"

### 5. Recovery Rally Blindness
**Symptoms:** VIX peaked at 55, now 42, still selling ITM calls
**Reality:** When VIX declines 10+ points from peak, recovery imminent - short calls will destroy you
**Coach response:** "VIX declining 13 points. Playbook says STOP SELLING. Why are you still covered?"

## Coach Accountability Questions

### Every Monday (Required Check-in)
1. "What's current VIX? ____"
2. "What phase does that put you in? [Normal/Stressed/Crisis/Recovery]"
3. "What does the playbook say to do? [Specific strike]"
4. "Did you execute the trade? [YES/NO]"
   - If NO: "Why not? What emotion is driving this decision?"
5. "What premium did you collect? $____"
6. "Current LEAPS delta? ____"
   - If <0.72: "Cash reserves available? Do you need to roll?"

### During Market Stress (VIX >25)
1. "VIX is elevated at ___. Are you still executing or have you paused?"
   - If paused: "Remember: This is when the strategy pays most. What's the fear?"
2. "Have you calculated total position P&L?" 
   - Premiums collected: $____
   - LEAPS current value: $____
   - Net: $____ (usually still positive)
3. "Are you thinking about going to cash?"
   - If yes: "Google cost you $48k. What makes this different?"

### When Strategy Drift Appears
1. "You mentioned [new strategy]. Will this beat $300-400/week passive income?"
2. "How many hours will this require vs 10 minutes Monday morning?"
3. "Your 2025 lesson: Every distraction cost money. Is this worth repeating that pattern?"

### When Considering Exit
1. "What's triggered this? [Fear/Boredom/New idea]"
2. "What does your position P&L actually show?" (Force calculation)
3. "How much DTE remains on your LEAPS? ___ days"
   - If >180: "You have 6 months for recovery. Why exit now?"
4. "If you go to cash and market recovers 20%, how will you feel?"

## The Bottom Line for Coach

Will has a **proven, profitable system** that fails only when he deviates. His edge is systematic execution during periods when emotions scream to stop. The coach's job is to:

1. **Enforce Monday execution** - Did you trade? If not, why?
2. **Catch emotional decisions early** - "That sounds like fear/boredom talking"
3. **Remind him of past costs** - Google, April, summer diversions
4. **Validate when he's right** - "You followed the system. That's the win."
5. **Challenge deviations hard** - "Show me the math that beats turtle"

**The only way Will fails is if he stops executing.** The coach exists to prevent that failure pattern from repeating.
